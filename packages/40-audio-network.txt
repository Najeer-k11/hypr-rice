# title: Audio, network, bluetooth
# required: yes
# about: System services the bar modules and settings pages talk to. Skip any you already have set up differently.
pipewire | Audio server.
pipewire-pulse | Lets PulseAudio apps (most apps) play through PipeWire.
pipewire-alsa | Lets ALSA apps play through PipeWire.
pipewire-jack | Lets JACK (pro-audio) apps play through PipeWire.
pavucontrol | Volume mixer window (right-click the volume in the bar).
networkmanager | Network service. rice-wifi and rice-settings use its nmcli tool.
network-manager-applet | Tray icon, and the connection editor (nm-connection-editor) for VPNs / static IPs.
bluez | Bluetooth service.
bluez-utils | Provides bluetoothctl, used by rice-bt and rice-settings.
